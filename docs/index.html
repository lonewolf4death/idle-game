    Automation

    Configure generator buyers and automatic Condensing.

    Unlock a buyer in the skill tree, then enable it here. Basic buyers attempt the next pattern; advanced buyers purchase Max. Timers use real seconds, including time out of focus. Failed attempts are not saved up. When timers coincide, Condense has priority, then buyers act in generator order.

    Enter a whole number of 1 or more. Scientific notation is supported, for example 1e3. Auto-Condense pauses while you edit this field.

    Auto-Condense waits until the available reward meets your Quanta target, then claims the full reward on its next attempt. The normal 1e50 Energy minimum still applies. Automatic Condenses skip the cinematic. Basic buyers start enabled when unlocked; other toggles keep your saved choice.
