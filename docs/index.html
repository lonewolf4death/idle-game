Configure generator buyers and automatic Condensing.

Unlock a buyer in the skill tree, then enable it here. One purchase per attempt. Timers use real seconds, including time out of focus; clicks do not accelerate them. Failed attempts are not saved up. When timers coincide, buyers act in Gen order, then Condense.

Enter a whole number of 1 or more. Scientific notation is supported, for example 1e3. Auto-Condense pauses while you edit this field.

Auto-Condense waits until the available reward meets your Quanta target, then claims the full reward on its next attempt. The normal 1e50 Energy minimum still applies. Automatic Condenses skip the cinematic. New automation starts disabled.

Spend them on permanent perks and Mass. Mass strengthens Energy, adds 10% to Breaks per order of magnitude, and at 1e25 forms the first stable atom.
