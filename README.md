# Cosmic Idle

**[Play Cosmic Idle](https://lonewolf4death.github.io/idle-game/)**

A space-themed incremental game about forming structure from primordial Energy.

Begin with 100 Energy to purchase Gen 1. Energy production is entirely generator-driven; clicking the field grants nothing. Develop generators and complete purchased resonance patterns. Patterns follow a repeating 4 / 6 / 8 / 12 phrase. Each pattern has one unit price until completion; the next pattern raises that price. Completion strengthens the generator and echoes a smaller bonus into the previous generator. Added units benefit without filling patterns. Choose single purchases, Next pattern, 5, 10, 100 or Max; use 1–9 and 0 for generator hotkeys. Next pattern buys one SGen unit in the Symmetry layer.

Gen 1 produces continuously from its first unit. Gen 2 builds stable strength, with rolls applying +20% or -20% around it; QR contributes a logarithmic bonus outside the purchased-count exponent. Gen 4 purchases immediately increase base Energy output, then deliver normalized growth each second. QR and ER also pay fractional amounts every second; the former QR interval resistance now reduces each payout, preserving its progression curve. Gen 5 retains a dependable baseline between its timed peaks. Prices grow more steeply after 32 completed generator patterns to preserve late progression.

Stabilize the Field to rebuild with remembered structure. The first four Stabilizations unlock Gen 4–7; there is no Stabilization cap. Later refinements provide further rebuilding strength and require 1e100, 1e400, 1e1600 and increasingly distant Energy goals. This lower reset keeps permanent progression and the complete Condense-run duration. Its confirmation lists reset boundaries, and recovery statistics track the return to the previous Energy high. Condense remains the major prestige at 1e50, awarding Primordial Quanta and access to the skill tree. Condense history provides stronger Energy, while every normal Condense run starts with zero Stabilizations. Rhythm no longer grants a starting Stabilization. Opening Quanta choices are discovered in the tree. Shift-click an available skill node to purchase directly. The static 2D perk map reveals owned skills and their connected next choices. The Condense tab appears after the first Condense; the footer action handles the first reset. Automation and individual Challenges appear only when usable. Unavailable achievements stay hidden; earned achievements remain visible. Echoes have a hover/focus tooltip. Labels use sentence case, retaining proper resource and mechanic names. Perk 4-1 is a one-Quanta Resonance foundation; early routes have lower costs for the Stabilization economy.

Basic automation unlocks for all seven only after the first Condense, never from Stabilization. These buyers attempt one next pattern every 15 real seconds. Eight repriced skills (10–50 Quanta) upgrade Gen 1–7 to Max buyers and unlock auto-Condense, with saved individual/all toggles in Automation. Advanced buyers start at 5 seconds and ten challenge completions improve them to 1 second. Auto-Condense has a saved minimum Quanta reward target, takes priority over simultaneous Max buyers and claims the full reward. Challenges require their advanced automation skill and permanent Energy milestones. They pause automation and Stabilization; entry and exit restart the run while keeping permanent progress.

The early economy is benchmarked from a fresh save and a first-Quanta repeat run. Timings depend on purchasing policy, rolls and player choices. Perks formerly tied to clicks now strengthen passive production, form added generators or reinforce support generators. Gen 7 coupling scales with purchased Gen 1s. Early Quanta choices and later capstones were repriced using seeded simulations; late pattern resistance preserves the 1e1000 pacing target. Achievement, pattern, Fluct, Stabilization, Quanta and production contributions appear in Stats. Tooltips, settings and reset descriptions reveal only known mechanics. Continuous production creates bounded, fading glimmers without manual Energy clicks. Energy remains comma-separated below 1e25; scientific notation is compact above that point.

Symmetry unlocks for 10,000 Quanta. Switch generator layers beneath the title. Field Alignment starts at 1e1000 Energy; its generator number is shown only in local dev reveal. Alignment capacity and shared resistance prevent large Energy balances from immediately completing Symmetry. Achievements do not boost its output. Milestones remain hidden until reached. Unified Interaction uses Energy prices anchored when 50% is first reached. Condense resets current Symmetry and Field Alignment but preserves Unified purchases and discoveries. Symmetry multiplies Energy by 10^(100*S^4), applying after late Energy resistance.

At 94%, convergence slows. The 94%→100% approach takes about 30 minutes at current formation capacity, before Perfect Alignment or Asymmetry speed bonuses, at game speed ×1. The 95%→96%, 96%→97%, 97%→98%, 98%→99%, 99%→99.9% and 99.9%→99.99% stages take 20, 40, 80, 160, 320 and 640 seconds at capacity. The entry stage takes 20 seconds and the finite 99.99%→100% stage takes 520 seconds. Fewer Field Alignments take longer. Perfect Symmetry adds ×100 to Condense Quanta, multiplying the existing 25% milestone and other rewards.

Break Symmetry unlocks at 95%. Base Asymmetry reward is floor(20^((S−0.95)/0.05)), from 1 at 95% to 20 at 100%. Reward upgrades apply before rounding. Breaking resets current Symmetry, purchased/added Field Alignment and every Unified Interaction purchase. It keeps Energy, Primordial progression, Quanta, skills, discoveries, achievements, lore and time. New Unified prices anchor to retained Energy. A separate Asymmetry page has six repeatable upgrades: formation speed, Quanta gain, Energy, gentler accelerating Field Alignment costs, Asymmetry rewards and starting Symmetry. Asymmetry and these purchases survive lower resets.

Perfect Alignment at 90% extends the existing discoverable Condense map, with three speed perks, two starting-Symmetry perks and five reward/production perks. IDs 5-1 through 5-5 cost 1e5, 3e5, 1e6, 3e6 and 1e7 Quanta, granting ×1.5 formation, +10 percentage points of starting Symmetry, ×2 convergence, +25 starting percentage points, and ×3 formation. IDs 5-6 through 5-10 cost 1e5, 5e5, 2e6, 5e6 and 2e7, granting ×2 Quanta, ×1e20 Energy after resistance, ×2 Perfect Symmetry Quanta, ×1.5 Asymmetry rewards and ×5 Quanta. They connect to the existing Rhythm and Continuum routes. The map fits horizontally and scrolls vertically. The two starting bonuses add to 35%. Residual order starts at 5 Asymmetry, costs ×4 per next level, and adds 2 starting percentage points per level (five levels, +10 points maximum). Combined, the three upgrades can start resets at 45%. Each starting-Symmetry purchase also adds its newly gained percentage points to current Symmetry immediately, capped at 100%, using the normal milestone/lore/achievement path. Future Condenses, Stabilizations, challenge restarts, respecs and Breaks use the full starting amount. Reloads never repeat purchase grants. Respec removes its refunded Perfect Alignment bonuses while retaining Asymmetry upgrades.

Newly discovered tabs, layers, generators, skills, shops, automation and challenges pulse until interacted with; acknowledgement is saved. Settings can independently choose Eventide, Celestial score, Glass observatory or Redshift audio, and the original Deep Signal visuals or Spectral lattice. Both preserve the original Condense music/cinematic. Stabilization aligns the field into a retained pattern; Break Symmetry fractures it into a brief blackout. Reset visuals can be disabled and respect reduced motion. The new field artwork uses the existing bounded 20-fps renderer, with no independent ambient animation/audio loop.

Progress saves in your browser. Settings includes Export, Import and rolling recovery backups. Failed loads and conflicting tabs pause autosaving instead of replacing stored progress. Asset URLs are versioned to avoid mixed cached modules. Older saves retain achievements, lore, skills, resources, generator counts, settings and earned generator stages when migrating to resonance patterns. The hosted site and a local file have separate browser storage; use Export/Import to move progress.

Auto-Stabilize unlocks after the first Condense and starts disabled. Its interval averages all other unlocked buyer intervals, including auto-Condense; disabled buyers still count. It supports individual/all toggles and later refinements. Ready auto-Condense rewards take priority. Challenges, reset confirmations and editing the Condense target pause it. Background catch-up and development time skips continue across automatic Stabilization.

Offline progress uses a saved activity timestamp and a return panel for unprocessed gaps of ten seconds or more. Choose accurate simulation, faster approximate checkpoints, or skip the remaining time. Completed progress is retained when skipping; active challenges use accurate mode. The panel reports Energy balances, earned Quanta and automatic resets without revealing undiscovered systems. Pending autosaves preserve the unprocessed cursor, preventing duplicated rewards or lost pending time on refresh. Time already processed in the background is not counted again.

Fifteen additional lore fragments follow echoes, generator rhythms, skill choices, repeated Condenses, challenges, Symmetry and Asymmetry. Discoveries and read acknowledgements survive resets. Automatic lore uses the deduplicated nonmodal notification queue; Lore replays discovered fragments. Seven new achievements grant modest rewards for lifetime QR, a first purchased Field Oscillation, purchased units of every Primordial generator, ten Condenses, exactly five purchased Field Alignment levels, Perfect Symmetry, and a first Break. Perfection remembered grants ×1.025 Symmetry speed after reaching 100%, applied after formation limits and throughout convergence. Other Energy/generator achievement rewards do not affect Symmetry speed.

## Public edition

The standalone [Animation & sound gallery](https://lonewolf4death.github.io/idle-game/gallery.html) previews fifteen current effects without loading a save or starting the simulation. It reuses the actual renderers and styles, with play, pause, replay, a seekable timeline, independent visual/audio loops, both visual directions and four audio comparisons. Samples can play alongside any visual. Break Symmetry now spreads mirror cracks from an off-center impact, holds thirty-six reflective shards, then tilts and drops them through a star-lit fracture into a blackout. A glowing Asymmetry remnant survives the 7.5-second sequence, accompanied by its own tension, glass-impact and unresolved-remnant score. Reduced motion retains a short gentle fade. Settings has saved individual animation controls and an All animations toggle; unknown effect names remain hidden until discovered. Animation toggles leave sound preferences unchanged. Condense preserves its original cinematic and score. Glass observatory uses FM bells and airy pentatonic motifs; Redshift uses warm analog pulses, low bass and darker chords. Settings also provides the same short audio sequence for every direction, a Stop control and a gallery link. Selecting a direction is saved, while gallery controls never touch game saves.

The Energy screen's footer Condense action performs the reset directly and keeps the current view. Lore, achievements and milestone notifications share a nonblocking queue at the top right. Click anywhere on the card, or focus it and press Enter/Space, to dismiss with a two-second fade. The optional six-second automatic dismissal applies to the shared queue. Lore acknowledgements are saved immediately, and pending lore survives run resets without duplicates. Read discovered entries again in Lore.

The current game is in docs/, deployed by the GitHub Pages workflow. The public edition contains no developer reveal control, development multipliers, Quanta grants, time skipping or development-only respec. Sound starts after your first interaction and can be adjusted in Settings. No installation, external assets or backend are required.

The older root scaffold remains in the repository; Pages serves only docs/.
